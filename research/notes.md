# Source notes

- Source date: 2026-10-04
- Public idea: [Marathon bib finder for runners who lost the lottery](https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/ideas/marathon-bib-finder-for-runners-who-lost-the-lottery-927b0337)
- Newsletter summary: major-marathon lotteries leave many runners without entries while participating charities hold fundraising places. The suggested wedge is a live board of open charity places filterable by race, minimum, and deadline, starting with one race and free supply onboarding.
- Public page access: the public URL redirected to sign-in after the Vercel security checkpoint, so no subscriber-only scores, keyword metrics, pricing ladder, or detailed GTM fields were copied. This prototype relies on the public URL and a short newsletter summary only.
- Reported signals (not independently verified here): 240,000+ applications for the 2026 NYC Marathon drawing; roughly 670 participating charities; a reported $80M raised by charity runners in the prior year. Treat these as research leads, not audited facts.
- All listings, dates, place counts, charities, and availability in the app are explicitly fictional demo fixtures.
